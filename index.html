House rules

How a drop works · how buying works

The drop

  • Each lot hangs with its price published ahead, and offers are taken while it hangs.
  • The queue hears drop dates first, and gets first refusal when a lot goes unsold.
  • An offer at or above reserve that we accept takes the lot off the floor.
  • A lot that does not sell goes back into stock, is improved, and is listed again at a new price.

The purchase

  • Offer — email us from the button on the lot. Questions are answered before money is discussed.
  • Agreement — the sale is written down in plain words: what you get, what is licensed, the price.
  • Payment — transfer happens when payment has cleared, by the method named in the agreement.
  • Transfer — the domain moves to your registrar account and the site's files are handed over working. The apps run on the Apex engine under your licence, at the rates on Lot № 1.
  • Running costs — yours from day one: hosting (the store is static files and runs on free tiers) and the domain renewal at the registrar's standard rate. We promise no traffic and no income, on any lot, ever.
